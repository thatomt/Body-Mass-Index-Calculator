Important: Understanding Your BMI

BMI is a screening tool and does not directly measure body fat or determine an individual's overall health.

Pregnancy

BMI is highly inaccurate during pregnancy because pregnancy-related weight changes make standard BMI calculations unreliable.

Bodybuilders and muscular individuals

BMI can be highly inaccurate for bodybuilders because it does not distinguish muscle mass from body fat, potentially classifying muscular individuals as overweight or obese.
